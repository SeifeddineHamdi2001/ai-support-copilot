# Support SLA and Uptime Commitment

Acme Cloud's service level agreement covers support response times and platform uptime.

## Support response times

The first response time depends on the customer's support tier:

| Support tier | First response  | Hours         |
| ------------ | --------------- | ------------- |
| Standard     | within 24 hours | business days |
| Premium      | within 8 hours  | business days |
| VIP          | within 4 hours  | 24/7          |

Urgent tickets about a full outage are answered within 1 hour for every tier. Response time is measured
from ticket creation to the first reply from a human agent; automated acknowledgements do not count.

## Uptime commitment

Acme Cloud commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the dashboard and API on Business and Enterprise plans.
Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance on the status page is excluded.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, Business and Enterprise customers can request service
credits:

- below 99.9%: 10% of the monthly fee
- below 99.0%: 25% of the monthly fee
- below 95.0%: 50% of the monthly fee

Credits must be requested within 30 days after the end of the affected month and are applied to the next
invoice. Starter and Pro plans are not covered by the uptime commitment.
