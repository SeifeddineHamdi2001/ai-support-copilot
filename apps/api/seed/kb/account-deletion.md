# Cancelling a Subscription and Deleting an Account

Cancelling a subscription and deleting an account are separate steps.

## Cancelling a subscription

An account owner can cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. The plan stays active until the
end of the current billing period and does not renew. After that the account moves to a read-only free
state for 90 days, so the customer can still export their data.

Cancelling does not trigger a refund on its own; see the Refund Policy for refund eligibility.

## Deleting an account

Only the account owner can delete an organisation, from Settings > Organisation > Delete organisation. The
owner must type the organisation name to confirm.

- Deletion is a soft delete for 30 days: during that time support can restore the organisation at the
  owner's request.
- After 30 days all projects, members and files are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
- Backups containing the data are purged within a further 60 days.
- Invoices are kept for 10 years for legal accounting reasons, even after deletion.

Export all data before deleting the account; Acme Cloud cannot provide an export after permanent
deletion.

## Removing a single user

To remove one member instead of the whole organisation, an admin removes them from Admin > Members. Their
personal data can be erased on request under the GDPR process.
