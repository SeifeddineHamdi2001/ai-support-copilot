# Billing FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about invoices, payment methods and charges.

## Invoices

### When are invoices issued?

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period: the day of the month the subscription
started for monthly plans, or the purchase anniversary for annual plans. Each invoice is emailed to the
billing contact and is available under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

### Changing the company name or address on an invoice

Update the company name, address and VAT number under Settings > Billing > Billing details. Changes apply
to future invoices automatically. To reissue a past invoice with the new details, the billing contact can
contact support with the invoice number (for example INV-2026-1043); reissued invoices keep the same number
with a revision suffix.

### VAT and tax

Customers in the EU who enter a valid VAT number are billed without VAT under the reverse-charge rule.
Without a VAT number, VAT is charged at the rate of the billing country.

## Payment methods

Acme Cloud accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Annual Business and Enterprise plans can
also pay by bank transfer on a 30-day invoice.

To change the card, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and add the new card before removing the old
one. The new card is used for the next charge.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Acme Cloud retries the card 3 times over 7 days and emails the billing contact after
each attempt. If all retries fail, the account becomes read-only until the balance is paid. No data is
deleted while an account is read-only.

## Charges I don't recognise

Card statements show charges as "ACME CLOUD". If a charge looks wrong or appears twice, contact support
before opening a dispute with the bank: duplicate charges are refunded in full (see the Refund Policy).
Opening a chargeback suspends the account until the dispute is resolved.
