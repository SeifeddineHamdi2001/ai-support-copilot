# Refund Policy

Acme Cloud wants customers to pay only for the service they use. This policy explains when a refund is
possible, how it is calculated, and how long it takes to reach the customer.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions (Starter, Pro and Business billed monthly) can be refunded in full within 14 days of
the charge. After 14 days the current month is not refundable, but the customer can cancel so that the
plan does not renew.

A refund within the 14-day window is granted once per account. Repeated refund requests for the same
account are reviewed by the billing team.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date.

After 14 days, annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis: the customer receives the value of the
remaining full months, minus any discount that was applied for paying annually. The partial current month
is not refunded.

Downgrading from an annual Business plan to a cheaper plan converts the difference into account credit
instead of a refund, unless the customer asks for a prorated refund.

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge (the same plan billed twice for the same period) is always refunded in full, no matter
when it is reported. Duplicate charges are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business
days after support confirms the duplicate.

Support confirms a duplicate by matching the two charges in the billing history. The customer does not
need to send a bank statement.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Acme Cloud cannot refund to a different card or a
bank account. Card refunds take 5–7 business days to appear on the statement, depending on the bank.

## What is not refundable

- Usage-based add-ons (extra API calls, extra storage) already consumed.
- Months that ended before the refund request, except for duplicate charges.
- Accounts closed for violating the Terms of Service.
