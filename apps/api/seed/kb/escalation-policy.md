# Escalation Policy

When a support agent hands a ticket to a more senior team, and how.

## Escalation levels

- **Tier 2**: technical issues the first-line agent cannot solve with the knowledge base, bugs that need
  reproduction, and tickets close to breaching the SLA.
- **Specialist**: security incidents, suspected data breaches, data protection (GDPR) requests, and billing
  disputes or chargebacks. These go to the security, privacy or billing specialist team.
- **Manager**: legal threats, a customer mentioning a lawyer or contract termination, repeated contact
  without resolution from a VIP customer, and complaints about an agent.

## When to escalate immediately

- The customer mentions legal action or a lawyer: escalate to a manager, do not discuss liability, and do
  not promise compensation.
- The customer reports a security incident (account takeover, leaked data): escalate to the security
  specialist and advise the customer to rotate their credentials.
- A VIP customer is blocked: escalate to Tier 2 even if a workaround exists.

## How to escalate

Add an internal note with a summary of the problem, what was already tried, and the customer's tier. Then
set the ticket status to Escalated. The receiving team acknowledges within 1 hour during business hours.
The original agent stays in copy and keeps the customer informed until the receiving team replies.
